# Changelog

## 1.1.0

Firefox support, alternatives and exclusions, and a lot of polish. Everything from the two betas below:

- **Firefox** (140 or newer): the same extension, built from the same source.
- **Parameters as one list**: Required, alternatives in a "Match at least N of M" frame, and Must not have — each from
  the mode menu of a parameter. Conditions the extension cannot edit are kept, searched unchanged and open in the
  original.
- **How parameters work**: the info button next to Your filters explains Total, Explicit and Implicit and the three
  modes.
- **A range in one row**: a second handle on the slider for a maximum; every parameter has its colour and icon.
- **Add parameter** browses every parameter the item can have (or the whole catalog with no item), by source.
- **More filters**: the site's rarer filters under the parameters; a set one shows in Your filters as a compact row.
- **Listings**: infinite scroll (a new search starts at the top); click the price to sort by price.
- **Saved searches**: a name already saved asks to replace it or keep both; deleting asks first.
- Category pictures in the category list; notices close with their button or the next edit.

## 1.1.0-beta.2

The second beta: Firefox joins Chrome.

- Firefox (140 or newer): the same extension, built from the same source; requests go to the trade site as the page.
- The chosen category picture shows beside the list where the browser has no picture list (Firefox); thin scrollbars
  there too.

## 1.1.0-beta.1

A beta for testers.

- Parameters as one list: Required, alternatives in a "Match at least N of M" frame, and Must not have — each from the
  mode menu of a parameter. Conditions the extension cannot edit are kept, searched unchanged and open in the original.
- How parameters work: the info button next to Your filters explains Total, Explicit and Implicit and the three modes.
- A range in one row: a second handle on the slider for a maximum, both bounds and the stat in one field.
- Every parameter has its colour and icon; Life is red.
- Add parameter browses every parameter the item can have (or the whole catalog with no item), by source, with counts.
- More filters: the site's rarer filters (item level, quality, requirements, corrupted, seller…) under the parameters;
  a set one is shown in Your filters as a compact row with its value to edit and a cross to remove it (with Undo).
- Category pictures in the category list.
- Infinite scroll: the next listings load as you near the end of the list; a new search starts at the top.
- Click a listing's price to sort by price, low to high and back.
- Saved searches: a name already saved asks to replace it or keep both; deleting asks first.
- Notices close with their button or the next edit.

## 1.0.0

The first release.

- The trade search page as two panes: filters on the left, listings on the right, the width adjustable; on a narrow
  window the filters open as a panel over the listings.
- Filters named by the item ("Find a ring"): category, rarity and base, a budget in a currency, and only the parameters
  you add, each with Total, Explicit or Implicit, a minimum and an optional maximum.
- Add parameter: one search over the trade stats, popular and recent first, compatibility with the chosen item shown.
- The site's own item cards: rarity frames, sockets and runes on the icon, mod tiers, roll ranges on hover, DPS and
  base percentile; searched parameters marked in their colour; click a line to sort by it.
- Compare two listings: price, searched parameters and the other properties side by side.
- Saved searches; Back and Forward through your searches; unsearched changes survive a reload; open them in the
  original site.
- Direct whisper, Travel to hideout and Copy whisper.
- One search per Find items; results of earlier searches shown again without a request; a countdown when the site asks
  to wait.
